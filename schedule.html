Course Schedule

Daily topics and readings for Summer 2026

This course runs from July 6–31, 2026, meeting Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday. Wednesdays are non-meeting reading days for catching up, working on readings, and office hours.

Required Materials

Each meeting day has three required materials:

  1. Oatley chapter. Oatley, Thomas. International Political Economy. 7th edition. Routledge, 2023. One chapter per class day; Chapters 6 and 7 are paired into a single combined session.
  2. Scholarly article. A peer-reviewed article or book chapter that extends or applies the day’s textbook material. Article citations are listed below; PDFs are posted to Canvas.
  3. Podcast episode. Typically from Trade Talks (Soumaya Keynes & Chad Bown). A short, accessible companion to the day’s material that surfaces contemporary policy debates.

Each student will sign up on Day 1 to present and lead discussion on one scholarly article. Day 1 (July 6) is reserved for course logistics and has no second article; I will model the article presentation on Day 2 (July 7) so you have a clear example.

Complete all assigned materials before the class session in which they are discussed.

In-Class Writing & Quizzes

  • Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays: Each class includes a short (~10 min) in-class writing activity tied to the day’s material. Timing within the session varies. Graded credit / no-credit (√, √+, √–).
  • Fridays of weeks 1–3: Class ends with a quiz covering that week’s material.
  • Friday, July 31: Cumulative final exam in class.
