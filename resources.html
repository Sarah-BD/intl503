Recommended Background Reading

If you want to dive deeper into IPE beyond the textbook:

Accessible Books

  • Frieden, Jeffry. Global Capitalism: Its Fall and Rise in the Twentieth Century
  • Irwin, Douglas. Free Trade Under Fire
  • Rodrik, Dani. The Globalization Paradox
  • Slobodian, Quinn. Globalists: The End of Empire and the Birth of Neoliberalism

More Academic

  • Eichengreen, Barry. Globalizing Capital: A History of the International Monetary System
  • Goldstein, Judith, et al. The Oxford Handbook of the Political Economy of International Trade
  • Simmons, Beth. Who Adjusts? Domestic Sources of Foreign Economic Policy
